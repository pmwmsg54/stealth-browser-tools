### FAQ

**What's the main reason to use an anti-detect browser?**
The primary purpose is to manage multiple online accounts safely by creating a unique and separate browser environment for each one. This method helps prevent platforms from linking your accounts, significantly reducing the risk of being flagged or banned, which is critical for e-commerce, social media marketing, and online research.

**Can these browsers be automated?**
Yes, most leading anti-detect browsers are designed for automation. They typically offer API access that integrates with popular frameworks like Selenium, Puppeteer, and Playwright, enabling you to automate repetitive tasks such as logging in, posting content, or gathering data.

**How do I choose the right anti-detect browser?**
Your choice should depend on your specific needs. Consider factors like your primary use case (e.g., marketing vs. data scraping), your budget, the size of your team, and any technical requirements like API access. Some tools are built for beginners with user-friendly interfaces, while others offer advanced features for large-scale enterprise operations.
